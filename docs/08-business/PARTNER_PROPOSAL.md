# Proposal to taxi operator — Technology & Automation Partner

## Client problem
500-vehicle operator has retrospective visibility, delayed driver cash/report settlement and investor obligations due on the 10th. Operational friction arises from repairs, downtime, driver compliance, taxes/inspections and manual reconciliation. Goal: automate standard work; report exceptions, forward risk and economic value.

## Our responsibility
Map processes and source systems, configure existing Planfix, connect accounting/bank/dispatch feeds, build independent cash intelligence, approval-bound AI assistance and an executive dashboard. We do not run the taxi company or take responsibility for driver operations/tax filings by default.

## Operator responsibility
Provide lawful, least-privilege access, field definitions, accurate source/contract data, staff/process owners, accountant/regulator review, security/privacy permissions and final approval of operations.

## Commercial possibilities — same product
A. Provider-owned 100% with minimum Starter subscription for pilot, optionally per vehicle / usage / commission / license / franchise / hybrid.
B. Joint technology venture, possibly 50/50, subject to separate contract on IP, development financing, product rights, revenue distribution, exit/deadlock and third-party sales.
One codebase; commercial toggle controls technology-product income only, and cannot alter operational truth or transfer IP.

## Pilot proof of value
Measure baseline and post-rollout: collection lag, reconciliation effort, 10th payout coverage warning lead time, vehicle downtime, unresolved exceptions, trust in dashboards, licensing/support costs. Avoid unverified savings claims or binding pricing before discovery.

## Safeguards
Source data stays under contractually agreed controller/processor terms. Production changes and sensitive actions have human approval. Pilot may begin with isolated historical/synthetic data.

## Decision requested after discovery
Select pilot funding, contract and owners; identify Planfix configuration, finance source and payments data; approve narrowly scoped test access.
