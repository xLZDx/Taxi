# Deferred candidate: small native Taxi CRM

Not in current pilot scope. Revisit only after measurable Planfix API, license, maintainability or UX constraints justify a total-cost-of-ownership study.

Possible concepts: vehicle/driver registry, investor/rental contracts, repair workflows, incidents, driver settlements, compliance, CRM communications and automation dashboard. A future native CRM would preserve the same integration/domain boundaries and operate behind adapters, not fork the product by customer.

Decision evidence required: operational Planfix audit, annual subscription cost vs engineering/hosting/security/support, migration and training cost, latency/limits, contract/legal risks, exit strategy. No coding or vendor replacement now.
