# ADR-0002: One auditable financial event foundation

Accepted 2026-10-16.
Decision: financial plan, internal management actual and legally prepared/statutory views derive from traceable evidence and reconciled recognition rules. EARNED, CONFIRMED, RECEIVED and CLEARED are distinct states. Sandbox cannot supply legal facts. No fake parallel books, hidden income or generated fictitious invoices.
Consequences: explicit reconciliation, accounting professional sign-off, immutable corrections and domain-specific access controls.
