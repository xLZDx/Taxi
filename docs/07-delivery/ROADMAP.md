# Roadmap — staged, evidence-driven

Durations are planning hypotheses, not delivery promises. Stage gates stop unsafe expansion.

## S0 — Foundation and customer discovery (weeks 1–2)
Repository and CI baseline; existing Planfix inventory; driver-to-cash contract map; 10th payout obligations; data ownership; security role map; KPI measurement. Output: scoped approved integration contract, no live mutations.

## S1 — Cash visibility pilot (weeks 3–5)
Controlled ingest from at least one earning feed and one receipt source in sandbox; distinct EARNED/CONFIRMED/RECEIVED/CLEARED states, source timestamps, dedup, exception backlog in Planfix, basic plan vs actual.

## S2 — Investor liquidity forecast (weeks 6–8)
Daily dates/obligations, 10th payout coverage, low/base/high scenarios, driver report lag, uncertainty bands, manager dashboard and escalation tests.

## S3 — Operating workflows (weeks 9–11)
Driver documents, repairs/downtime, supplier tasks, tax deadlines, inspections, risk controls, Planfix role-dependent workflows.

## S4 — Reliability and controlled live pilot (weeks 12+)
Restart/replay, bad-feed, duplicate, outage and sandbox/live segregation testing. Human acceptance, legal/accountant sign-off and a gradual vehicle-group rollout.

## Future tracks
AI assistant read-only / approval-bound, more integrations, multi-tenant self-service, proven commercial models, later evaluation of a native taxi CRM.

20% planned task contingency is built into internal schedule estimates, but immutable contractual/legal deadlines remain fixed.
