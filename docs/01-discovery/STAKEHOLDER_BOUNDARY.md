# Collaboration boundaries — clarified with investor

The technology provider is **also a private investor owning several cars in the taxi fleet**. This personal investment relationship is not authorization to manage the fleet's other vehicles, other investors, employees, bank data, accounting, customer records or Planfix configuration.

- **Taxi fleet director**: owner/operator of the taxi company, sole operational decision maker under its corporate rules. Wants simpler workflows, reliable money visibility, timely investor payments and fewer manual problems.
- **Technology provider**: offers a standalone product, Planfix optimization, integrations, structured processes and transparent forecasts. May be paid by subscription (pilot), by other configured commercial model or through a separately negotiated technology-venture agreement.
- **Drivers and fleet staff**: remain employees/contractors of the taxi operator; their rights and obligations are governed by the fleet's existing contracts and regulations.
- **Investors**: have their own legitimate payout/reporting interests, not automatic access to other investors' or drivers' private records.
- **Accountant/legal advisers**: review tax and statutory reporting mappings, financial evidence and regulatory implications. Technology itself does not certify filings.

Pilot ownership principle: the technology provider builds a lab with synthetic data and earns access to production only after the director authorizes narrowly scoped integration. The goal is operational improvements **for the fleet director**, not displacement of the taxi business.

Commercial question remains open: provider-owned product with subscription/commission vs negotiated 50/50 ownership **of the technology venture**. It must not affect fleet operating logic, user privacy, source facts or regulatory reporting.
