# Open questions for the first customer workshop

Already known: one operator with about 500 vehicles; investor payout date is the 10th; drivers sometimes finish reporting 1–2 weeks after month close; operator runs its own taxi business; existing Planfix use; technology provider intends subscription pilot.

Need validation:
- Are 500 vehicles operator-owned, leased, investor-owned, or mixed? Are there separate legal entities or brands?
- Which dispatch/ride-hailing providers, and can they provide signed, dated payout and ride events?
- How are cash vs card rides collected; who holds money; who owes whom under the contract?
- Which bank, cash systems, 1C/accounting products, fiscal records and currencies are authoritative?
- Exactly what are investor contracts: fixed rent, fixed date, percentage, minimum guarantee, deductions or grace periods?
- Are payout dates contractual and what happens on public holidays or banking delays?
- What is the driver settlement cadence; how are missing reports, reversals and disputes recorded?
- How far ahead can inbound cash be predicted, based on historical lag distributions?
- Which Planfix objects/automations currently exist and who can approve discovery access?
- What are local regulatory duties, legal basis for processing personal data and retention periods?
- How many office users need Planfix seats and how will drivers access a cheaper portal/bot?
- Which actions are safe to automate without approval?
- What is existing baseline loss from idle vehicles, delay, bad debt and manual reconciliations?
- Who signs off the scope, security model and pilot acceptance?

No assumptions from this list should become facts without collected evidence.
