# Business Plans & Real Reporting (BASELINE / Starter)

One reconciled evidence foundation produces business forecasts, actual internal performance views and legally grounded statutory extracts. Different views may use legitimate recognition timing, grouping and rules; every difference must be reconcilable and truthful. No fictitious parallel ledgers or hidden operations.

## Business Plans
- Forecast accrued/earned revenue, expected settlements by source, collections by delay distribution, fleet expenses, repair downtime costs and cash requirements.
- Prepare payout coverage horizon for contractual investor payments due on the 10th.
- Scenario models: optimistic / base / stressed; include missing-driver-report lag and overdue payments.
- Model scheduled task time +20% contingency, separate from cash reserve and bank clearing constraints.

## Real Reporting
- Show confirmed revenue, cash receipts, cleared bank funds, receivables/debts, commitments, exceptions, forecast vs actual and freshness timestamp.
- Internal management P&L and cash flow with explicit basis (cash, accrual, contractual) and documented reconciliation to accounting source.
- Statutory exports only from verified authorized source records and accountant-approved mapping/rules; filing is a separate approved act.
- Append correction trails; avoid silent overwrite and never recast estimates as receipts.

## Example (hypothetical MDL, not customer data)
Driver economic activity 20,000 MDL; verified collected receipts 14,000 MDL; 6,000 MDL remains uncollected, assuming validated receivable. Planning may estimate the collection date, but liquidity counts only cleared available money. The driver’s 20,000 economic activity is not by itself proof of 20,000 cash.

## Planfix division
Planfix owns tasks, process states, owners, approvals and escalation. External finance module owns time-aware cash math, evidence, forecast and reconciliation. Accounting system remains authoritative for official postings/tax declarations.
